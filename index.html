FAQ

Questions clinic owners ask me

How fast will I see results?

Leads usually start coming in soon after campaigns go live. The first few weeks are about learning what offers and audiences work in your market, and that's when I make the biggest changes. After that it's steady weekly improvement. I won't promise overnight miracles, but you'll always know where things stand.

What ad budget do I need?

It depends on your city, your treatments and how many new patients you want each month. I'd rather give you a straight answer for your situation than a generic number, so that's one of the first things we'll cover on the audit call. If your budget is too small to get meaningful results, I'll tell you.

How do you handle follow-up with leads?

This is where most clinics lose money, so I take it seriously. I set up automated SMS and email that goes out right after someone inquires, plus call tasks for your team, nurture sequences for people who aren't ready yet, and confirmations and reminders to cut down on no-shows. Usually this runs in GoHighLevel, and I can work with your current setup too.

Which treatments do you work with?

Mostly med spa and aesthetic treatments: injectables like Botox and fillers, skin treatments, laser, body treatments, and wellness services. I've also run campaigns for other healthcare clinics. If you're not sure your treatment is a fit, just ask.

How does reporting work?

You'll get clear, plain-English updates on the numbers that matter: leads, contacts, bookings, show-ups and new patients, plus what each of those cost. Not just clicks and cost per lead. I'll also tell you what I'm changing next and why.
